# Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons – tekster

Upload `ImageShot-extension-<version>.zip` fra udgivelsen. Kategori: *Productivity*. Privatlivspolitik: `docs/PRIVACY.md` (skal ligge på en offentlig adresse, fx GitHub Pages).

**Begrundelse for tilladelser (Privacy practices):** activeTab/scripting – fotografere den side, brugeren vælger; downloads – gemme billeder; clipboardWrite – kopiere billeder; storage – indstillinger; contextMenus – højrekliksmenu; valgfri <all_urls> – kun ved "Alle faner". Ingen fjernkode, ingen dataindsamling.

**Skærmbilleder:** brug 1280×800 – fx `draw-editor.png`, `crop-editor.png`, `full-long.png` fra grenen ci-shots-extension.

## en

**Kort beskrivelse (≤132 tegn):** ImageShot – screenshots with scrolling, drawing and cropping

**Lang beskrivelse:**

Capture exactly what you need – the visible area, a region, the whole page or all tabs.

• Full page: scrolls and stitches long pages, also inside scrollable boxes
• Region: drag a frame – push its edge to scroll while selecting
• All tabs at once (optional permission)
• Draw: arrows, rectangles, ellipses, highlighter, text, step numbers
• Blur or pixelate passwords and personal details
• Crop before copying or saving
• PNG, JPEG or WebP with an easy quality slider
• Keyboard shortcuts, right-click menu, light and dark mode
• English, Danish, German and Spanish

Private by design: no account, no tracking, nothing leaves your computer.

Privat fra starten: ingen konto, ingen sporing, intet forlader din computer.

• Español, inglés, alemán y danés
